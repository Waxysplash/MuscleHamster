# MUSCLE HAMSTER — VISUAL BRAIN

---

## Identity

**Brand Name:** Muscle Hamster

**What we do:** We help people get fit with low guilt.

**Brand Feel:** Fresh, Fun, Warm, Playful

**Boundaries:** Never aggressive. Never pushy. Muscle Hamster is an encouraging friend, not a drill sergeant.

---

## Audience

**Who they are:** Everyday people — any age (18-60), any background, any fitness level. Anyone who wants to get moving and likes a cute way to do it.

**Their frustration:** They're not moving enough. They know they should be more active but they're not doing it.

**What they actually want:** To feel more fit and build a habit of moving — without the pressure or intimidation of traditional fitness apps.

**Visual preferences:** Warm tones — beige, orange, cream. Nothing dark, cold, or intimidating. Inviting and soft.

---

## Voice Rules

### Always
- Short, punchy sentences
- Gentle nudges, not demands
- Reference the hamster as your buddy/pal
- Keep it warm, encouraging, and fun
- Tie CTAs back to the hamster companion

### Never
- Commanding or authoritative tone
- Guilt-tripping or shaming
- Aggressive fitness language
- Pushy or high-pressure messaging

---

## Colour System

**Palette:**
- `#4A3728` — Primary dark background (dark brown)
- `#FFF8F0` — Primary light background (cream)
- `#8B5A2B` — Headlines and emphasis (warm brown)
- `#FF9500` — CTAs and highlights (orange)
- `#6B5D52` — Secondary text
- `#E5DDD5` — Borders and dividers

**Usage Rules:**
- Carousels alternate light (`#FFF8F0`) → dark (`#4A3728`) → light
- Headlines in `#FF9500` orange on dark slides, `#8B5A2B` warm brown on light slides
- Body text in `#FFF8F0` cream on dark slides, `#4A3728` dark brown on light slides
- When text overlays an image, use `#4A3728` overlay at 60% opacity
- Never use cold blues, greys, or neon colours

---

## Typography

**Headlines — Quicksand Bold**
- Weight: 700
- Size: 36–48px
- Case: Sentence case

**Subheadings — Quicksand SemiBold**
- Weight: 600
- Size: 24–32px
- Case: Sentence case

**Body — Nunito Regular**
- Weight: 400
- Size: 18–22px
- Case: Sentence case

**CTA — Nunito Bold**
- Weight: 700
- Size: 20–26px
- Case: Sentence case

**Text Rules:**
- Max 8 words on a hook slide
- Max 15 words on a body slide
- Never below 18px
- Always sentence case — all caps is too aggressive for this brand

---

## Imagery Direction

**Aesthetic:**
Warm, earthy, cozy, and inviting. Content should feel like a gentle nudge from a friend, not a gym ad. Golden hour lighting, soft warm tones, approachable spaces. The hamster character is the visual anchor across all content.

**Image Sources:**
- Hamster character illustrations (primary brand element)
- Mix of stock/AI photos — cozy workout spaces, yoga mats in living rooms, uncrowded gyms, golden hour scenes

**Colour Treatment:**
- Warm vintage grade with golden tones
- Photos should feel soft and inviting, never harsh or overexposed

**Off-Brand (never use):**
- Muscular/bodybuilder imagery
- Corporate stock photos
- Close-ups of muscles or bodies
- Neon or overly bright colours
- Sexualized imagery
- Intimidating gym environments

## Layout and Composition

**Dimensions:**
- Instagram square: 1080x1080
- Instagram portrait: 1080x1350
- Stories/Reels: 1080x1920
- Twitter/X: 1600x900
- TikTok: 1080x1920
- Twitch banner: 1200x480
- Twitch overlay: 1920x1080

**Text Positioning:**
- Centred by default on all slide types

**Margins and Safe Zones:**
- Minimum 80px padding on all sides
- Stories/Reels: 120px top and bottom for UI overlap

**Text on Image:**
- Overlays allowed using `#4A3728` at 60% opacity
- Never place text over the focal point of an image

**Density:**
- Generous breathing room
- Maximum 3 elements per slide
- When in doubt, remove something

---

## Visual Rhythm

**Background Pattern:**
- Light → dark → light (alternating)
- Hook slide always opens on light

**Image Pattern:**
- Images on every slide

**Energy Arc:**
- Consistent throughout — warm and steady from start to finish

---

## Content Architecture

**Carousel: 3 slides**

- **Slide 1 — Hook (light):** Max 8 words. Quicksand Bold headline. Hamster or warm photo. Grab attention.
- **Slide 2 — Value (dark):** Max 15 words. The tip, insight, or main point. Cream text on dark brown.
- **Slide 3 — CTA (light):** Designed, not empty. Clear call to action. Nunito Bold in `#FF9500` orange.

---

## Brand Marks

**Handle:** Muscle Hamster 🐹
**Position:** Bottom centre on every piece
**Sign-off:** "Take care of your buddy. Take care of you."
**Mascot:** Happy hamster character on every piece — small near the handle or featured prominently depending on the slide
